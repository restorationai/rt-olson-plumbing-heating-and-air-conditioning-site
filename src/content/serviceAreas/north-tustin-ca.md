## Plumbing and HVAC emergencies common in North Tustin

North Tustin's housing stock skews older and larger-lot than much of surrounding Orange County, which means a lot of original plumbing and mechanical systems are still in service well past their expected lifespan. Water heaters installed decades ago start rumbling with sediment or leaking at the tank seam without warning. Cast iron and galvanized drain lines under ranch-style slabs narrow with scale over the years, so a slow kitchen sink or a gurgling tub often signals a bigger blockage downstream, not just a clogged trap. Mild winters here mean freeze damage is rare, but the mix of heavy clay soil and seasonal downpours during our wet months can shift slab foundations slightly over time, stressing old pipe joints and showing up as a damp spot on a slab floor or a water bill that jumps for no visible reason.
