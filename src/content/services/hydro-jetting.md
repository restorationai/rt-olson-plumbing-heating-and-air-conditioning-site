A kitchen line that backs up every few months no matter how many times it's been snaked usually isn't a clog problem, it's a buildup problem. Grease, soap scale, and mineral scale coat the inside of the pipe in layers, and a cable auger just punches a hole through the middle without touching what's stuck to the walls. Hydro jet drain cleaning uses high pressure water to strip that buildup back to bare pipe, which is why it's the fix contractors reach for when a drain keeps coming back after a basic snaking.

## What Hydro Jet Drain Cleaning actually involves

Hydro jetting sends water through a specialized hose and nozzle at pressures far beyond what a garden hose or standard snake can generate, scouring the full diameter of the pipe rather than drilling a single path through a clog. The nozzle pulls itself forward through the line while spraying water backward and to the sides, which cuts through grease, soap scum, mineral scale, and root hair as it travels. For most residential lines this means a kitchen stack, laundry line, or single bathroom group can be jetted in under an hour; a full main sewer line jetting job, especially one with root intrusion, usually runs longer because the technician works the nozzle back and forth to clear the full length before confirming flow. This isn't a tool used blind. A camera inspection beforehand tells us what we're dealing with, pipe material, condition, and where the actual blockage sits, so the jetting pressure and nozzle type get matched to the line instead of guessed at.

## Our process

1. **Camera inspection.** Before any water pressure goes into the line, we run a camera through it to confirm pipe material, locate the blockage, and check for cracks, bellies, or offset joints that jetting could make worse instead of better.
2. **Pressure and nozzle selection.** Cast iron, PVC, and clay lines each tolerate different pressures. Older or already-compromised pipe gets a gentler setting and a different nozzle than a sound modern line with heavy grease buildup.
3. **Jetting pass.** The hose feeds in and the nozzle works downstream, scouring grease, scale, and root intrusion off the pipe wall. On longer or heavily blocked lines this usually takes more than one pass.
4. **Flow verification.** Water is run through the line and, where needed, the camera goes back in to confirm the pipe is clear end to end, not just unclogged at the point we could see.
5. **Findings review.** If the camera shows a structural issue jetting can't fix, bellied pipe, a collapsed section, root damage at a joint, we walk through what that means before recommending repair.

## What separates a good hydro jetting response from a bad one

The most common mistake is jetting without a camera inspection first. Running full pressure into a line with an unseen crack or a bellied section can turn a clog into a bigger problem, pushing debris into a low spot that then re-clogs within weeks. A careful technician matches pressure to pipe condition and age instead of running one setting on every job, and knows when a line needs root cutting attachments versus a standard grease-cutting nozzle. Another gap shows up with grease line jetting specifically: clearing the visible grease without identifying what's causing repeat buildup, whether that's a failing grease trap, an undersized line, or a slope problem, means the homeowner is back to square one in a few months. A thorough job documents before-and-after camera footage so there's a clear record of what was cleared and what the pipe looks like afterward.

## Seasonal & regional considerations

Corona and the surrounding Inland Empire sit on a mix of older clay sewer lines and newer PVC, and clay in particular is vulnerable to root intrusion from mature trees along established streets. Dry summers followed by sudden rain can also shift soil around older sewer laterals, which shows up as recurring slow drains even when nothing visible has changed at the fixture. Homes with mature landscaping or properties on older infrastructure tend to need root-clearing jetting more often than newer builds on straight PVC runs.
