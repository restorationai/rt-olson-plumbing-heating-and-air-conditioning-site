When a kitchen line backs up for the third time in a year or a sewer lateral keeps slowing no matter how often it's snaked, hydro jetting is usually the step that actually resets the pipe instead of buying a few more weeks.

## Why Fullerton Properties See Recurring Drain Problems

Fullerton sits in a part of Orange County with clay-heavy soil that shifts slightly with the seasonal wet-dry cycle, and that movement is hard on older sewer laterals, especially ones with multiple joints from decades of partial repairs. Add in mature street trees, common throughout the city's older residential blocks, and you get root systems that find their way into clay pipe joints looking for moisture. A standard cable auger cuts a channel through those roots, but the channel closes back up within months because the roots and the grease and scale coating the pipe walls are still there. Hydro jetting clears the full diameter of the line, not just a path through the middle of it.

## Our Hydro Jet Drain Cleaning Process in Fullerton

We start most jobs with a camera inspection so we know what the line is actually made of and where it's compromised before we put water pressure to it. That matters in Fullerton specifically because a lot of properties still have transitions between original clay pipe and later ABS or PVC repairs, and jetting at full pressure against a cracked or offset clay joint can make things worse instead of better. Once we know the pipe material and condition, we run a jetting hose down the line with a nozzle sized to the pipe diameter, working back toward the cleanout so grease, scale, and root mass get pushed out instead of packed further downstream. For kitchen and restaurant-adjacent lines dealing with grease buildup, we adjust nozzle pattern and pressure specifically for grease line jetting rather than treating it like a standard sewer lateral. A follow-up camera pass confirms the line is actually open wall-to-wall, not just unclogged at the point we were working.

## Local note

One thing we watch for specifically in Fullerton: a lot of the original orange grove-era lots were platted with long, narrow setbacks, which means sewer laterals on older properties often run longer distances from the house to the street connection than you'd see in newer Inland Empire subdivisions.
