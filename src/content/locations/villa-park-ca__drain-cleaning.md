## Why Villa Park Homes See Drain Clogs

Villa Park is one of the few cities in the area that's held onto its large-lot, semi-rural character, and that's part of what drives drain trouble. Big lots mean big trees, and Villa Park has plenty of mature oaks, eucalyptus, and sycamores whose root systems reach for any available moisture, including the condensation and slow seep around old clay sewer laterals. Homes from the city's main growth period in the 1960s and 70s were commonly plumbed with cast iron or Orangeburg pipe, both of which corrode or collapse from the inside over decades. Add in the region's hard water, which leaves mineral scale along pipe walls and narrows the usable diameter of a drain line year after year, and you get a city where a simple clogged drain often turns out to be a symptom of something further down the line.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Villa Park

We start with a cable or jetting pass appropriate to the fixture, whether that's a kitchen sink clog removal, a sluggish shower drain, or a full rooter service on a main line. For anything beyond a simple localized clog, especially on properties with older clay or cast iron runs, we recommend a sewer camera inspection before and after the cleaning. On a foothill lot where the line may run forty or fifty feet through root-heavy soil before reaching the street connection, a quick snake job can clear the immediate blockage without addressing a root mass that will regrow and clog the same spot again within months. Hydro jetting gives us a way to scour the full interior diameter of the pipe, cutting through grease and scale and clearing root intrusion more completely than cabling alone, which matters on the longer, older lateral runs typical of this city.

## Local note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Villa Park: a drain that only backs up after irrigation or a heavy rain is often a sign that tree roots have found a crack in the sewer lateral and are drawing in groundwater along with sewage, which bloats the root mass and worsens the clog seasonally.
