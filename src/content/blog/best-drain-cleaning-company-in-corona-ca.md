## What should you check before hiring a drain cleaning company in Corona?

Confirm three things before anyone touches your cleanout: an active contractor license, a clear answer on whether a camera inspection is included or billed separately, and a written quote before work starts. A verbal "it'll probably be fine" on a recurring clog is how a simple snaking call turns into a repeat visit three weeks later.

Ask specifically whether the technician plans to snake the line or jet it. A cable auger is usually enough for a single slow fixture. A line that backs up repeatedly, especially a main line serving the whole house, often needs [hydro jetting](/blog/hydro-jetting-vs-snaking/) to actually scour grease and root mass off the pipe wall instead of just punching a hole through the clog. Our breakdown of [drain cleaning pricing in Corona](/blog/drain-cleaning-cost-in-corona-ca/) covers what drives the cost difference between the two methods.

Also ask who's licensed to pull a permit if the job turns into a repair. Anyone can rent a drain machine; a CSLB license is what's actually required for plumbing repair work in California, and you can confirm any company's status in under a minute on the [CSLB's lookup tool](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/OnlineServices/CheckLicenseII/CheckLicense.aspx).

## How is a sewer camera inspection different from basic drain cleaning?

A sewer camera inspection feeds a waterproof camera through the line to show the pipe's actual condition, while drain cleaning is the physical work of clearing whatever is blocking it. One diagnoses, the other fixes. Running a camera first tells you whether you're dealing with a soft clog, a belly in the line holding standing water, or root intrusion through a joint, and that distinction decides whether the right next step is a quick snaking or a repair quote.

Skipping the camera step is the single biggest reason homeowners call back within a month for the same clog. If a drain backs up on the same schedule every few weeks, especially the lowest fixture in the house, it's usually not a cleaning problem anymore. If jetting alone hasn't held, it's worth scheduling a [sewer camera inspection](/services/sewer-camera-inspection/) to see what's actually happening below the line.
